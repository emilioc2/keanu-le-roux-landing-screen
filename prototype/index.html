Financial Guidance You Can Trust.

I'm Keanu Le Roux, a qualified financial advisor dedicated to helping individuals, families and business owners take control of their financial futures. My approach is built on trust, transparency and a genuine commitment to understanding your unique goals.

With experience across financial planning, wealth management, retirement strategy and risk protection, I work alongside you to build a plan that evolves with your life. Great advice starts with listening — and I'm here to make sure you always feel informed, confident and in control.

I believe financial planning should feel empowering, not overwhelming. Every conversation starts with your goals — your lifestyle, your family, your ambitions — and from there we build a strategy that's as unique as you are.

I focus on education, clarity and long-term thinking. No jargon, no pressure, just honest guidance you can trust.

I hold relevant industry qualifications and am an authorised financial services representative. My areas of expertise include wealth planning, risk protection, investment management, retirement planning and estate structuring.

You get a dedicated advisor who genuinely cares about your outcomes. I take the time to understand your full picture, communicate clearly, and stay with you as your life and goals evolve. Your success is my success.

What I Offer

Comprehensive financial services tailored to your unique needs and goals.

A holistic view of your entire financial life brought into one clear, personalised plan. By aligning your goals, lifestyle, cash flow, investments, protection and long-term strategies, I help you answer the most important question: how much is enough?

Protecting your income, family and assets against life's uncertainties. Through carefully structured risk solutions, I ensure that illness, disability or unexpected events don't derail your long-term financial plan.

Choosing and structuring the right healthcare solutions for your needs and stage of life, with a focus on affordability, appropriate cover and long-term sustainability.

Disciplined, goal-driven investment and savings strategies aligned to your objectives, timeframes and risk profile. Focused on long-term growth, diversification and consistency.

Planning for a financially secure and fulfilling retirement with a clear roadmap — from structuring your savings and investment strategy to income planning so you can retire with confidence.

Integrated planning for business owners that connects business strategy to personal wealth outcomes — from cash flow and succession to risk and investment planning.

Structuring your estate to protect your loved ones and preserve your legacy. Coordinating beneficiaries, estate structures and long-term intentions so your wealth is transferred according to your wishes.
