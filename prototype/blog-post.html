Why Starting Early Is the Best Financial Decision You'll Ever Make

Time is the most powerful tool in wealth building. The decisions you make in your 20s and 30s have an outsized impact on your financial future.

If there's one piece of advice I find myself repeating to clients, it's this: start now. Not next year, not when you earn more, not when things "settle down." The single greatest advantage you have in building wealth is time — and every year you wait, that advantage shrinks.

The Power of Compound Growth

Compound interest is often called the eighth wonder of the world, and for good reason. When your money earns returns, and those returns earn returns of their own, the growth becomes exponential over time. The earlier you begin, the more time compounding has to work in your favour.

Consider two people: one starts investing R2,000 per month at age 25, the other starts the same amount at age 35. By the time they both reach 60, the early starter could have nearly double the wealth — not because they invested more, but because they gave their money ten extra years to grow.

Small Habits, Big Outcomes

Starting early doesn't mean you need large sums of money. In fact, the habit of saving and investing consistently matters far more than the amount. Setting up a monthly debit order into a diversified investment portfolio — even a modest one — builds discipline and momentum.

Over time, as your income grows, you can increase your contributions. But the foundation you lay in your 20s and 30s is what makes the real difference.

What You Can Do Today

  • Set up an emergency fund covering three to six months of expenses
  • Start a tax-free savings account and contribute regularly
  • Review your employer retirement fund contributions — are you maximising the match?
  • Speak to a financial advisor about a personalised investment strategy
  • Automate your savings so it happens before you spend

It's Never Too Late — But Earlier Is Better

If you're reading this and thinking you've already missed the boat, you haven't. The second-best time to start is today. But if you're in your 20s or 30s and haven't yet taken that first step, you're sitting on an incredible opportunity. Don't let it pass.

Financial planning isn't about restriction — it's about freedom. The earlier you start, the more choices you'll have later. And that's what building wealth is really about: giving yourself options.
