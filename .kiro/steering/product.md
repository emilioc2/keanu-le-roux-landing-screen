# Product Overview

Keanu Le Roux Financial Advisor Website — a responsive landing page for a South African financial advisor. The site serves as a lead-generation and personal branding channel.

## Purpose
- Generate qualified leads via a contact form
- Build trust and credibility through professional presentation
- Showcase financial advisory services (wealth planning, risk protection, retirement, etc.)
- Promote thought leadership via blog content and social media feeds
- Grow a newsletter subscriber base (external signup link)

## Target Audience
- Individuals seeking personal financial planning
- Professionals and business owners interested in long-term wealth planning
- Existing and prospective clients following Keanu's content

## Key Sections
1. Hero (video background, dual CTAs)
2. About (bio, accordion with approach/qualifications/why)
3. Services (7 financial services in accordion)
4. Blog / Insights (featured post + card grid, CMS-driven)
5. Social Media Feed (YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram cards)
6. Newsletter CTA (external subscribe link)
7. Trusted Partners (static logo placeholders)
8. Contact Form (lead capture with success state)
9. Footer (contact info, social links)
10. Blog Post Detail Page (standalone article page)

## CMS
- Sanity CMS for blog posts only (Date, Title, Excerpt, Image, Body)
- All other content (logos, social links, services) is static
- Single admin user (Keanu) with Sanity built-in auth
